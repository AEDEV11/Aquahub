## Launch and account flow

```text
MainActivity → splash
  → onboarding when not completed
  → login when signed out
  → registration_flow(register → station_setup → subscription)
  → main_container when session/station is ready
```

Google registration also enters station setup. Demo mode creates a local session and seed data. Logout signs out and stops sync. When the sync queue is empty, it clears the Room cache and its recorded owner from this device, then reseeds shared plan data; cloud account data is unchanged and the completed-onboarding flag is retained so the next launch returns to login. When pending, failed, in-progress, or quarantined sync work remains, logout preserves the Room cache and owner scope so another account cannot access or replay it. The user must sign back into the original account to sync or recover that work before switching.

## Owner operations flow

`main_container` hosts dashboard, orders, inventory, customers and settings for owners/managers. Staff sessions are restricted to Home/Dashboard and Orders; the staff Home header provides sign-out, and staff station pulls are scoped to membership-assigned station IDs. Staff see a single Walk-in Station Sale FAB that opens POS directly. Owner/manager sessions retain the expanded FAB shortcuts for walk-in sales, products, riders and reports; the dashboard quick actions also expose the Business/Pro expense tracker. Rider sessions do not show a FAB. Additional root destinations open order/customer detail, products, riders, reports, expenses, station profile/management and subscription screens. Station profile's open/closed switch persists a manual override; while active, the switch takes precedence over the configured closing time and is cleared when the schedule is changed.

A manual refresh schedules synchronization and waits briefly; it is not a direct awaited end-to-end refresh result.

## Order business rules

Use `domain/usecase/order/OrderUseCases.kt` as transition authority. The implemented transition graph is documented in the workspace shared-contract file. Repository mutations update Room and enqueue sync together.

Customer-created Firestore orders are pulled into Room. Remote string customer/product/order IDs are resolved or mapped to local numeric IDs. The owner domain currently omits source, customer UID/profile ID, idempotency key, delivery mode, rejection/cancellation reasons, requested time and remote order version, so the UI cannot reliably distinguish/report all customer context.

Walk-in sale creation validates unique product lines, station/customer ownership and stock, decrements saleable product units, and updates customer order counts in an outer Room transaction. Paid pickup sales create a matching paid receipt; delivery walk-ins and client-account charges remain pending without a receipt until collection. Assigned riders may record a delivered-order collection only with a private proof image and a server-confirmed receipt. Customer detail derives paid amounts and balances from `PAID` ledger rows only, accepts partial or full receipts without overpayment, and keeps the aggregate order `PENDING` until integer-centavo receipts equal the full total. Legacy order-level paid flags without receipts are not treated as customer payments. Cancelled/rejected orders are excluded from account billing. Customer-owned refill bottles do not change station-container inventory. Explicit station-container loans/returns require a named customer and one refill size per sale; returns must not exceed that customer’s loan balance or station aggregate.

Dashboard revenue and its seven-day trend are based only on `PAID` receipt rows by `paidAt`/`createdAt`, not order status or order totals. A completed client-account charge therefore contributes zero until collection; each partial receipt contributes only its amount on the day received. Orders without paid receipt rows are excluded from collected sales.

## Inventory and fulfilment

Product stock is a per-product integer. Reusable container inventory is a separate size-label aggregate (`totalOwned`, `available`, `withCustomers`, `damaged`, `lost`) plus movement records; its configured size rows are also the owner station's shared size catalog. Order lists and container movement history are exposed from Room through Paging 3 with a 20-row initial/page size and five-row prefetch distance, avoiding full-history UI loads while retaining Room invalidation after local writes. Product creation selects a saved size, and an owner can add a new zero-stock size from the product dialog. Inventory bottle adjustments select only saved product/container sizes, preventing free-form label mismatches. Logical station/customer/order links are not all database foreign keys. Inventory displays product stock automatically from the catalog without copying quantities into reusable containers. Adding a product defines a saleable SKU; only an explicit physical container acquisition adds station-owned bottles. Repository deductions reject shortages and overflow instead of clamping. Movement transactions roll back on failure, and aggregate totals are the sum of available, with-customer, damaged and lost buckets. Unattributed damage/loss adjustments remove on-site stock; customer loans/returns use the customer ledger. Sold containers are customer property and do not create return obligations. The current aggregate does not distinguish dirty empty bottles from cleaned or filled bottles.

Rider assignment records rider identity/name and status timestamps. Customer-created orders retain nullable delivery latitude/longitude through Firestore sync and Room; order detail can open that destination in Google Maps as a rider reference. Rider coordinates are stored but there is no real-time tracking pipeline. Delivery is an order concern, not a separate entity.

## Subscription rules

Plans, features, limits, an initial onboarding trial and history exist locally. Operational destinations recheck access while open; use cases guard creation, updates, delivery dispatch, inventory, expenses, report content and export formats. Daily expense and net-cash tracking is available only to Business and Pro. Starter includes delivery settings, rider assignment, and dispatch for one station with up to two riders; higher plans add station, rider, reporting, and analytics capacity. Creation checks and product/customer/rider inserts share a Room transaction. After downgrade, only the oldest station slots up to the plan limit remain operational; rider assignment also validates the plan’s rider slots. Immediate expiration, suspension, cancellation and grace deadlines fail closed. Remote subscription snapshots override newer local simulation data. Initial onboarding trial issuance and identity-level eligibility are server-authoritative through Supabase; the Owner app checks eligibility after account/station setup, provisions the selected paid plan's 30-day trial, and refreshes the Room replica before access gates evaluate it. Supabase's perpetual Free row uses status `FREE`; Android parses that value explicitly and resolves it as active only for plan code `FREE`, independent of the non-billing period timestamp. Unknown status values remain fail-closed. Subscription replicas are not replayed through the operational outbox. Owner billing now checks Play ownership before new checkout, requires a server preflight before paid replacement, and binds new purchases to the selected owned organization through Play profile identity. Explicit restore re-verifies owned tokens and reports current paid access only for an entitling Play state in the selected business; background reconciliation is silent. Free access never inherits Play ownership as paid entitlement, and existing Play subscriptions open Play management for payment, cancellation or renewal. Cancellation leaves paid access until its current period ends. These local billing changes are release-pending; trial/paid entitlement validation and a Play-signed end-to-end release test remain required before production activation.

## Reports

Report repository/use cases aggregate Room flows and exporters keep a temporary FileProvider-backed result for opening/sharing. The success dialog uses Android's folder picker to copy a generated report into a user-selected device folder without broad storage permission. Preset and custom calendar selections use the same half-open timestamp contract: the selected start date is included and the end boundary is midnight after the selected end date. Trend windows follow those exact calendar-day bounds and display at most the latest 30 days. Avoid scaling all-record in-memory aggregations without bounds. Some delivery classification remains address-text heuristic.

## Inventory workflow reference

Primo Brands distinguishes refilling customer-owned bottles from exchanging station-supplied reusable bottles: https://www.water.com/education/the-complete-guide-to-5-gallon-water-jug-care-storage-refilling-and-recycling/ . This supports keeping catalog/sale units, owned-container assets and customer loans separate; the application does not infer container acquisition or loans from product creation or refill selection. Water production in liters, consumables, bottle cleaning states, remote stock reservation and rider custody remain unimplemented.
