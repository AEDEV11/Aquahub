MGA MADALAS NA TANONG

Clear answers before you get started.

Learn how AquaHub Owner and station-linked mobile ordering fit together.

Who is AquaHub for?

AquaHub Owner is the Android business app for water-refilling station teams. Participating stations can offer customers a browser-based mobile-ordering link.

Does AquaHub work without an internet connection?

Many Owner workflows are local-first and synchronize when connectivity returns. Mobile ordering requires an active station link and its online ordering service.

Which plans include container tracking?

Basic container movement tracking is included in eligible paid plans. Station teams manually record acquisitions, loans, returns, damage, or loss; product setup does not infer bottle deposits or exchanges.

Gumagawa o nagbebenta ba ng drinking water ang AquaHub?

Hindi. Software platform lang ang AquaHub. Ang bawat water-refilling station ang responsable sa water treatment, permits, pricing, at fulfillment.

How do I confirm plan price and billing?

The plan amounts on this page are the current intended catalogue prices. Where a paid plan is offered, check Google Play for the available plan, exact checkout price, billing period, renewal, and cancellation terms. This website does not process subscriptions.

Puwede bang mag-request ng account at data deletion?

Oo. Available ang account deletion sa loob ng apps o sa aming Account Deletion page.
