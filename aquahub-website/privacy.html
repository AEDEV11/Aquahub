Privacy Policy

This policy explains how AquaHub Owner, AquaHub Customer, and related services collect, use, protect, retain, and disclose personal information.

1. Scope and operator

AquaHub is a digital platform designed to help water-refilling station owners manage business operations and allow customers linked to a station through its QR code or link to view products, place orders, and manage delivery or pickup transactions.

This Privacy Policy applies to AquaHub Owner, AquaHub Customer, and AquaHub websites or services that link to this policy.

2. Information we collect

Account information

Depending on how you use AquaHub, we may collect name, email address, phone number, authentication identifiers, profile image if provided, account type, and account creation or update timestamps.

AquaHub Customer information

  • Saved delivery addresses and geographic coordinates associated with them.
  • Water station selections, cart items, products, quantities, order totals, delivery or pickup preference, status, history, delivery instructions, ratings, and feedback where available.
  • Notification information needed to send order and service updates.

AquaHub Owner information

  • Station name, business contact information, station address and location, business hours, product catalog, pricing, inventory records, customer records associated with the station, order and sales records, rider or staff information entered by the owner, container tracking information, analytics, reports, subscription status, and application settings.

Transaction and subscription information

We may process product quantities, order amounts, delivery fees, order status, payment method or status, transaction identifiers, and subscription entitlement information. Complete payment-card credentials may be processed directly by the applicable payment provider rather than stored by AquaHub.

Technical information

Depending on enabled services, AquaHub may receive device type, Android version, app version, application identifiers, crash information, diagnostic information, network status, performance information, and security-related information.

Marketing subscriptions

If you independently subscribe to AquaHub marketing updates or opt in from AquaHub Owner, we process your email address, optional first name, consent choice and time, consent-policy version, subscription source, confirmation state, and withdrawal or provider-suppression state. Newsletter sign-up requires an explicit unchecked-by-default consent choice. Creating an AquaHub account alone does not subscribe you.

3. How we use information

We may process information to create and authenticate accounts, provide AquaHub services, manage orders, connect customers with stations, support pickup and delivery, calculate distance, manage products and inventory, manage riders or delivery operations, generate reports and analytics, synchronize supported data across devices, maintain offline-first functionality, validate subscriptions, send service notifications, provide support, improve stability, prevent abuse, and comply with applicable law.

When you have opted in, we use your email address to send AquaHub product updates, helpful tips, and promotional offers. You can withdraw this marketing consent at any time through the unsubscribe link in a marketing email or the marketing preference in AquaHub Owner. Marketing consent does not control essential account, security, or service messages.

4. Legal basis for processing

Where applicable, processing may be based on consent, performance of a requested service or contract, compliance with legal obligations, legitimate interests that do not override your rights, or another lawful basis permitted under applicable law.

5. Location information

AquaHub Customer may request location permission to select a delivery address, retain map coordinates, calculate approximate distance, and check delivery coverage for the linked station. AquaHub Owner may use station location information to register a station location and support delivery-area features.

AquaHub does not intend to continuously track a user's device location in the background unless a future feature expressly requires this and the appropriate disclosure and permission controls are implemented.

6. Offline storage and synchronization

Some AquaHub functionality may operate offline. Information may be stored locally on a user's device and synchronized with AquaHub cloud services when connectivity becomes available. Users should protect access to their devices because locally stored information may remain accessible to someone who gains unauthorized device access.

7. Service providers

Depending on the feature, AquaHub uses Supabase for account authentication, database, server functions, and related backend services; Firebase services for push notifications and app diagnostics; Google Maps Platform for maps and location features; and Google Play for app distribution and digital subscription billing. When you independently opt in to AquaHub marketing emails, your subscription details may be synchronized to Hostinger Reach to manage marketing contacts, confirmation, campaigns, and suppression. Hostinger Reach is not used to replace essential account-verification or service-email workflows.

8. When information may be shared

AquaHub does not sell users' personal information. Information may be disclosed where reasonably necessary to complete an order between a customer and the selected station, coordinate an assigned delivery, operate through contracted service providers such as Supabase, Firebase, Google, and (for opted-in marketing subscriptions) Hostinger Reach, protect AquaHub or its users, investigate fraud or abuse, comply with valid legal requests, or meet applicable legal obligations.

When a customer places an order, relevant order and delivery information may become available to the station responsible for fulfilling that order.

9. Water station owner responsibilities

AquaHub Owner enables station owners to process information about their own customers, employees, riders, or business contacts. Owners are independently responsible for ensuring that their use of personal information complies with applicable law and that they only collect and use information for lawful business purposes.

10. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information, which may include secure authentication, access controls, Firebase security rules, encrypted network connections, server-side authorization, restricted cloud resources, subscription entitlement validation, and security monitoring. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

11. Data retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide AquaHub services, maintain accounts, process orders, maintain legitimate transaction records, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and establish or defend legal claims. Information no longer reasonably required may be deleted, anonymized, or securely disposed of.

Marketing consent records and suppression information are retained as needed to honor your preference, prevent future promotional sends after withdrawal, and document consent. Hostinger Reach may retain its own contact and campaign records under its retention terms.

12. Account and data deletion

Users may request deletion through the AquaHub application where available, through the external Account Deletion page, or by contacting the privacy address listed below. Some information may be retained where necessary or permitted for legal, financial-record, security, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes.

Deleting an AquaHub account does not necessarily cancel an active Google Play subscription. Subscriptions should be cancelled separately through Google Play where applicable.

13. Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to be informed about processing, access your personal information, request correction, object to certain processing, request deletion or blocking, request portability where applicable, withdraw consent where processing is consent-based, and lodge a complaint with the appropriate authority.

To stop promotional emails, use the unsubscribe link included in each marketing email or turn off the marketing preference in AquaHub Owner. You can also contact us using the details below. Essential account and service notifications remain separate.

14. Children

AquaHub is designed primarily for water-refilling station businesses and customers capable of entering into transactions. AquaHub is not intentionally directed toward children below the minimum age required to independently consent to the processing of their personal information under applicable law.

15. Third-party services

AquaHub may contain or rely on third-party maps, payment services, authentication services, and other resources. Their privacy practices are controlled by those third parties and users should review their policies where relevant.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as AquaHub develops or legal and technical requirements change. The Last Updated date identifies the latest version. Material changes may also be communicated through the app or other reasonable methods.
