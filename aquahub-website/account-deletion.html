Account & Data Deletion

How AquaHub Owner and AquaHub Customer users can request deletion of their account and associated personal information.

Deletion guideIn-app deletionWithout the appWhat is deletedWhat may be retainedSubscriptionsContact

1. Delete your account in the app

  1. Open AquaHub Owner or AquaHub Customer.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Select Account.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Review the deletion information.
  6. Confirm the deletion request.
The production app should implement a real account-deletion workflow. Do not publish this step until the feature actually exists.

2. Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have access to AquaHub, email adriel.dev.123@gmail.com with the subject AquaHub Account Deletion Request.

Please include the email address associated with your AquaHub account. We may request reasonable information necessary to verify account ownership before deletion.

3. What may be deleted

Depending on the account and how AquaHub was used, deletion may include:

  • AquaHub account profile and authentication account.
  • Contact information and saved customer profile information.
  • Saved delivery addresses.
  • Notification identifiers and app preferences.
  • Other personal information associated with the account that is not required to be retained.

4. Information that may be retained

Certain information may be retained when reasonably necessary or legally required for fraud prevention, security, legal compliance, dispute resolution, financial or transactional recordkeeping, or establishing, exercising, or defending legal claims.

Some completed order records may need to remain within a station's business records. Where appropriate, personal information may be deleted, minimized, or anonymized while required transactional information is retained.

5. Water station owners

Deleting an AquaHub Owner account may affect access to station information, inventory, orders, customers, reports, rider management, and subscription features. Owners should export any records they are legally required to retain before requesting deletion.

6. Google Play subscriptions

Deleting your AquaHub account does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription.

  1. Open Google Play.
  2. Go to Payments & subscriptions.
  3. Select Subscriptions.
  4. Select AquaHub.
  5. Cancel the subscription if you no longer want it to renew.

7. Processing time

We aim to process verified deletion requests within a reasonable period. We may contact you if additional verification is required.

8. Questions

For account-deletion or privacy concerns, contact adriel.dev.123@gmail.com.
