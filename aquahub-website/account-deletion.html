Account & Data Deletion

How AquaHub Owner users can request deletion of their account and related personal information.

Deletion guideIn-app deletionWithout the appWhat is deletedWhat may be retainedSubscriptionsContact

1. Delete your AquaHub Owner account in the app

  1. Open AquaHub Owner and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Select Delete AquaHub Account.
  4. Review what will happen, then continue and confirm Delete Account.
  5. If asked, sign in again to verify your identity.

The app will not start deletion while sale, payment, or inventory changes are still waiting to sync. Sync those changes first. Deletion removes the sign-in and profile and clears the app's local account cache after the server confirms deletion.

2. Request deletion without the app

If you cannot use the app, email adriel.dev.123@gmail.com with the subject AquaHub Account Deletion Request. Start an account deletion request

For a browser order without an AquaHub account, contact the same privacy address with your order number and the contact details used for the order. The station may need to participate in verifying and handling the request.

Include the email address associated with your AquaHub Owner account. We may ask you to verify ownership before processing the request. Do not send your password, one-time passcode, or purchase token by email.

3. What is deleted

When the request completes, the current AquaHub Owner deletion flow:

  • Deletes the AquaHub sign-in account and its linked Owner profile.
  • Removes your identity from audit records and clears the app's local account cache.
  • Withdraws an active AquaHub Owner marketing subscription and queues removal from the email provider.

4. Information that may be retained

Station and business records—including products, inventory, orders, payments, expenses, and reports—are not deleted by the current Owner account flow. The Owner profile is unlinked from its organization, so deleting the account removes your access and does not transfer station ownership. Contact support before deletion if another person needs access to those records.

For orders where the deleted account was recorded as the customer, associated contact, delivery, and note fields are removed or replaced with generic values. Financial and transaction records may be retained for business continuity, security, dispute resolution, legal or accounting obligations, and legal claims.

If an active Owner marketing subscription exists, AquaHub withdraws consent and requests provider removal. A limited email suppression/consent record may remain to honor the withdrawal and prevent future promotional sends. The provider may take time to complete a queued removal.

AquaHub does not currently publish a fixed retention period for records that must be kept. They are retained only while a stated business or legal purpose applies. Contact the privacy address below to ask about a specific record.

5. Water station owners

Deletion unlinks the Owner profile from its organization. It does not delete or transfer that station's business records. Make arrangements with AquaHub support before requesting deletion if station records need to remain accessible to someone else.

6. Google Play subscriptions

Deleting your AquaHub account does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription.

  1. Open Google Play.
  2. Go to Payments & subscriptions.
  3. Select Subscriptions.
  4. Select AquaHub.
  5. Cancel the subscription if you no longer want it to renew.

7. Processing time

The in-app request runs when you confirm it and the server accepts it. Email requests require ownership verification and support processing. AquaHub has not set a fixed completion period for email requests; we will contact you if more information is needed. Provider removal of a marketing contact is queued and may complete later.

8. Questions

For account-deletion or privacy concerns, contact adriel.dev.123@gmail.com.
