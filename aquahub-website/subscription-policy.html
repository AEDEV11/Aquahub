Subscription, Billing & Refund Policy

This policy applies to paid digital subscription features offered through AquaHub Owner.

Effective date: September 14, 2026Last updated: October 10, 2026 at 2:00 PM PHT (UTC+8)
On this pagePlansDaily estimatesFree trialPaymentRenewalRefundsPrice changes
Important: The Google Play checkout price is authoritative. The catalogue amounts below are intended reference prices, not a live checkout quote or confirmation that a plan is currently offered. Keep this page synchronized with the actual Play Console products, base plans, offers, and plan entitlements before launch.

1. Available plans

When paid plans are offered, the intended AquaHub Owner monthly catalogue amounts are:

  • Starter: ₱199/month
  • Business: ₱499/month
  • Pro: ₱999/month

A Free plan is also part of the Owner catalogue. Annual billing is planned with a 20% catalogue discount, but availability and checkout amounts are not verified by this page. Exact purchase prices may vary due to taxes, promotions, currency, Play Console configuration, or pricing updates.

2. Daily price estimates

AquaHub may display informational daily estimates based on a 30-day month. At the intended monthly catalogue amounts, the estimates are Starter ≈ ₱6.63/day, Business ≈ ₱16.63/day, and Pro ≈ ₱33.30/day. Users are not billed daily. The actual billing amount and billing period displayed at checkout control the purchase.

3. Annual subscriptions

Where annual billing is available, the intended catalogue uses a 20% discount relative to paying the monthly rate for twelve separate months. The full annual amount charged should be displayed before purchase. The website's annual totals are catalogue estimates; checkout details control any purchase. Any monthly or daily equivalent is informational only.

4. Free trial

Eligible new owners may start a selected paid plan with a 30-day AquaHub-managed trial. Under this model, AquaHub records the trial entitlement without opening Google Play checkout and without creating a Google Play charge at signup. When the trial ends, AquaHub asks the owner to activate the paid subscription through Google Play.

Google Play controls the final paid price, payment method, renewal terms, cancellation, and any eligibility rules that apply to a Play offer. Deleting an AquaHub account or clearing Firebase data does not reset a Google Play account's introductory-offer eligibility.

5. Payment

Subscriptions offered within the Google Play-distributed version of AquaHub Owner may be purchased and processed through Google Play. AquaHub does not directly receive or store complete payment-card credentials used by Google Play.

6. Automatic renewal and cancellation

Subscriptions may automatically renew unless cancelled before the next renewal according to Google Play's applicable rules. Cancellation normally prevents future renewal but may not terminate access immediately. Premium access may continue until the end of the already-paid billing period, depending on the applicable subscription terms.

Uninstalling AquaHub or deleting an AquaHub account does not automatically cancel a Google Play subscription.

7. Refunds

Purchases processed by Google Play are generally subject to Google Play's applicable refund procedures and consumer-protection requirements. AquaHub does not guarantee a refund merely because the app was uninstalled, the user forgot to cancel, the user did not use all available features, or the user changed their mind. Nothing in this policy limits a refund or remedy required by applicable law.

8. Failed or expired payments

If a payment fails, expires, is reversed, or is otherwise no longer valid, premium access may be restricted according to the verified subscription entitlement. AquaHub may support an applicable grace period where configured through Google Play.

9. Upgrades and downgrades

Users may be able to change plans. Billing treatment may depend on the current subscription, new subscription, billing period, and Google Play proration or replacement settings. Applicable purchase information should be shown before confirmation.

10. Feature entitlements

Paid features are available only while the relevant entitlement remains valid. Feature limits and plan capabilities may change as AquaHub evolves, provided changes are communicated where required.

11. Price changes

Subscription prices may change in the future. Price changes will be handled according to applicable Google Play rules and applicable law.
